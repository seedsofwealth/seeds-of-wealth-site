About Us

Building financial skills in Durham youth with heart and honesty

Our Story and Mission

Seeds of Wealth operates as 17460724 Canada Foundation, a federally incorporated nonprofit registered in Ontario, focused on closing youth financial gaps in Durham Region. We empower youth to understand, grow, and own their financial futures.

Through culturally relevant, hands-on programs, we equip young Canadians with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to make smart financial decisions, explore alternative assets, and cultivate an ownership mindset.

Seeds of Wealth is founded and led by Executive Director Quincy James, and is not yet a registered charity with the CRA — charitable status registration is currently in progress.
