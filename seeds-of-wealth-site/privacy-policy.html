Privacy Policy

Please read this policy carefully to understand how we handle your information.

Seeds of Wealth ("we," "us," or "our") operates seedsofwealth.ca as a not-for-profit service offering resources on community wealth-building. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information in compliance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial laws, including Ontario's privacy rules.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal information necessary for site operation, such as name, email, and contact details, when you subscribe to updates, submit a form, or contact us. Website analytics may capture IP addresses, browser type, and usage data via cookies, but no sensitive data like financial or health information.

No data is collected from children under 13 without parental consent.

How We Use Information

Personal information supports service delivery, like sending updates or responding to inquiries, and improves site functionality through analytics. We do not sell, trade, or use data for commercial purposes, in keeping with our not-for-profit mission.

Data retention is limited to what's needed; inactive subscriber data is deleted after two years unless required by law.

Cookies and Tracking

Essential cookies enable site features; optional analytics cookies track usage anonymously and can be declined via browser settings. Third-party tools (such as our contact and volunteer form provider) may set cookies if you interact with embedded content.

Disclosure and Sharing

Information is not shared with third parties except service providers (e.g., email and form platforms) under strict agreements, or as required by law. No international transfers occur without safeguards.

Your Rights and Access

You can access, correct, or delete your information by emailing info@seedsofwealth.ca. Opt out of communications anytime via unsubscribe links. Complaints about privacy handling go to our Privacy Officer (same email), with escalation to the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if unresolved.

Security and Updates

We use reasonable safeguards like encryption and access controls. This policy may be updated from time to time; changes will be posted here with notice for material revisions. The governing law is Ontario, Canada.
