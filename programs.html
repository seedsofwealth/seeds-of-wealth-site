Our Programs

Hands-on financial education, for every age and stage

Six programs, built for ages 6–25, delivered in schools, libraries, and community centres across Durham Region.

Ages 6–10

Mind on Money

Introduces young children to money through stories, play, and simple activities — coins and bills, needs vs. wants, saving, and sharing.

Ages 11–13

Money in Motion

Teaches pre-teens how to earn, budget, and make financial decisions through interactive activities, real-life simulations, and goal-setting.

Ages 14–17

Digital Wealth 101

Our flagship program. Introduces teens to online finance, investing basics, and entrepreneurship through interactive lessons and real-world simulations.

Ages 14–25

Value Vault

Introduces teens and young adults to alternative assets — art, trading cards, precious metals, collectibles, and crypto — through hands-on research and simulated investing.

Ages 18–25

Assets of Culture

Helps young adults understand and monetize cultural and creative assets — art, music, and digital content — through entrepreneurship and business planning.

Ages 10–17

Future Owners Camp

An intensive, hands-on entrepreneurship camp where youth plan, budget, market, and pitch a real business idea over a series of sessions.

FAQs

Common questions

Who can join?

Our programs are designed for young Canadians ages 6–25, delivered through school, library, and community partners in Durham Region.

What topics are covered?

Money basics, budgeting, saving, investing, alternative assets, and entrepreneurship — matched to each age group's stage of development.

How are programs delivered?

Programs combine hands-on activities, simulations, and culturally relevant lessons, typically delivered weekly over 4–6 sessions.

Is there a cost?

Many of our offerings are free or low-cost to ensure accessibility for all Durham Region youth.

How do I sign up?

Contact us or reach out through your school, library, or community centre to bring a program to your group.

Can parents get involved too?

Yes — we encourage family participation to support youth learning and financial growth at home.
