FAQs

Volunteering with Seeds of Wealth

How do I volunteer?

Fill out the form above to join our volunteer team, and we'll reach out with next steps.

Who can volunteer?

Anyone passionate about our mission and ready to help is welcome, including high school co-op students and post-secondary field placements.

What tasks are involved?

Volunteers assist with program facilitation, community outreach, event support, and spreading awareness.

Is training provided?

Yes — we offer guidance to help you feel confident in your role before your first session.

How do I get updates?

We keep volunteers informed through regular email updates.
