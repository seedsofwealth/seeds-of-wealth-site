Your Impact

Empower youth with financial confidence — donate today

Impact

Seeds of Wealth brings practical financial literacy to Durham Region youth through engaging programs at trusted partners like BGC Durham. Your gift covers curriculum, facilitators, and materials — no overhead waste.

Donations are not yet tax-deductible. Seeds of Wealth is a registered nonprofit, but our CRA charitable registration is still in progress, so we can't issue official tax receipts at this time.

How your gift helps

Where Funds Go

$150 covers one facilitator-led session. $900 runs a complete 6-week program cycle for a group. $1,500 covers a full program cycle including materials, marketing, and evaluation.

Your Role

By donating, you become part of a community that helps Durham Region youth build lasting financial confidence and an ownership mindset.
